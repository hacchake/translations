Said the Cabalist, Domus Sanctuarii queo est hic inferius , disponitur secimdum Domum Sanctuarii , quee est superitis : “ The Building of the Sanctuarie which is here below is framed according to that of the Sanctuarie which is above.” Here wee have two worlds, visible and invisible, and two universali natures, visible and invisible, out of which both those worlds proceeded. The Passive Universali Nature was made in the image of the Active Universali One, and the conformitie of both worlds, or sanctuaries, consists in the originali conformitie of their principies. There are many Platonicks (and this last centurie hath afforded some apish disciples) who discourse very boldly of the similitude of inferiors and superiors, but if we thoroughly search their trash, it is a pack of small conspiracies, namely, of the heliotrope and the sun, iron and the loadstone, the wound and the weapon. It is excellent sport to hear how they crow, being roosted on these pitiful particulars, as if they knew the Universali Magnet which binds this great frame and moves all the members of it to a mutual compassion. This is an humor much like that of Don Quixote, who knew Dulcinea but never saw her.
